Privacy policy

This draft privacy policy describes the personal information handled by the current website project for events, tickets, donations, contact, and administration. Foundation-specific policy details remain subject to confirmation and approval.

Who this policy applies to

This draft covers visitors, event attendees, ticket holders, donors, contact form users, and staff or admin users who access or use the Autism Allyship Foundation website and related project systems.

The website is run by Active Autism & Neuro-Diversity Allyship Foundation. Some legal and organisational details still require the Foundation's final wording and approval.

What personal information we collect

The current project supports the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact form information, such as name, email address, phone number and message.
  • Event and ticket registration details, such as name, email address, quantity, and ticket token information.
  • Donation information, such as donor name, email address, amount, message, and payment reference information.
  • Admin and staff account information used for sign-in and system access.
  • Information stored in Firestore for website content, tickets, contact submissions, donations, and related administration.
  • Technical information processed when the website uses Firebase, Cloudflare, or related infrastructure, subject to the relevant provider configuration.
  • The website may provide links to an external WhatsApp destination. Messages sent after following a link are handled by the relevant external service and the person's interaction with it; this website does not receive the message content through the link itself.

Why we collect it

We collect personal information to respond to enquiries, administer events and tickets, process donations, maintain the website and admin tools, communicate with supporters, and fulfil our legal and operational responsibilities.

Google Analytics is not implemented in the current project. The current project code is not intended to use advertising tracking or a cookie-based analytics system. This describes the code currently included in the project and does not confirm every future deployment or third-party configuration.

How we use it

The website is designed to use personal information for the tasks it supports, including:

  • To respond to contact messages and support requests.
  • To manage event registrations and validate booked tickets.
  • To send ticket or confirmation emails where the relevant email service has been configured.
  • To process and record donations and payment references.
  • To maintain the resource directory, event information, blog content and admin records.
  • To protect the security of the website, admin accounts and data in the project systems.

Retention and deletion

The project records the following retention periods. The Foundation must still approve a period for admin and staff account records before this policy is final:

  • Contact submissions are retained for 12 months.
  • Ticket records are retained for 12 months after the event.
  • Donation records are retained for five years.
  • Admin and staff record retention period: to be confirmed by the Foundation before launch.

This draft does not establish a Foundation retention or deletion policy. The final policy must explain the approved process for requests to correct or delete personal information.

Third parties and service providers

The current project includes or connects to the following services where they are relevant to the task being carried out:

  • Firebase and Google Cloud services for authentication, Firestore data storage, and related project infrastructure.
  • Cloudflare Pages and Cloudflare Workers for hosting, website delivery, file handling and automation.
  • Paystack for payment processing on the donation and ticket flow supported by the project.
  • Brevo may be configured for transactional email messages. The Foundation must confirm whether it processes personal information for the live website.

The Foundation must confirm the final circumstances in which personal information is shared with these providers. This draft does not establish a broader Foundation sharing policy or confirm every provider used in a live deployment.

Data processed outside South Africa

Some third-party technology providers may process information in jurisdictions outside South Africa, depending on their infrastructure and configuration. The project does not confirm the Foundation-specific processing arrangement or POPIA basis for any such processing.

The Foundation must confirm its cross-border processing arrangements and POPIA basis before launch.

Until that confirmation is approved, this section is a draft description rather than a final cross-border processing position.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013, a person may have rights to request access to personal information, ask for correction of inaccurate information, and ask for deletion or restriction where the law allows it.

The final policy will identify the approved contact route for requests to access, correct, or delete personal information. The Information Officer details below remain placeholders until the Foundation supplies and approves them.

This draft does not establish the Foundation's final request-handling procedure.

Contact the Information Officer

Information Officer name and contact details: to be supplied by the Foundation before launch.

Physical or postal address: to be supplied by the Foundation before launch.

Security and safeguards

The project uses Firebase Authentication for admin access, Firestore for data storage, and Cloudflare-hosted services for website delivery and related API endpoints. These technical components have their own configuration and access controls.

The Foundation must confirm any organisational safeguards, access rules, and final security commitments before publication. No website or service can guarantee that all security risks are eliminated.
